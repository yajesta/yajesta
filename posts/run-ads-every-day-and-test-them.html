Run ads every day, and test them

17 Nov 2026

Become a Trillionaire peaked at around 5,200 people in the game at once, and about 330,000 Robux a day. Ads were one of the ways people found it.

I ran a steady 20 to 40 ad credits a day. I started before launch, and I kept that going once the game was live. Starting early means you find out which ads get clicks before the day you want people to arrive. Keeping them on means new players can still find the game after that day has passed.

A burst can fill one day and leave the next one empty. I treated 20 to 40 credits as a range I could hold, not a number to spike and then drop to zero. If a day came in at the low end or the high end, that was fine. Stopping entirely was the thing to avoid.

The ads got better because I tested them. For a while the click-through rate was about 3 to 6 percent. Later it was about 12 percent. That did not come from one clever change. It came from running more than one ad, comparing the click rates, and dropping the weak ones.

How to test

Run two or three ads at a time, not one. If you only run one, you have nothing to compare it to, and a quiet day can look like "ads don't work" when the ad is the problem.

Change one thing at a time: the image, the line of text, or the hook. If you change everything at once, you cannot tell what moved the click-through rate.

Give each version enough time to get a real rate. A handful of impressions will jump around. Wait until you can see a stable difference, then keep the stronger ad and replace the weaker one. Repeat. That is how a 3 to 6 percent click-through rate becomes a 12 percent one: not in a single swap, but in a series of them.

Click-through rate is the first filter, not the whole story. An ad can get clicks and still send people who leave immediately. After you have a stronger click-through rate, watch whether those players stay and play. If they don't, the ad may be selling something the game is not.

Show the game. A still that looks like the real thing, and a line that says what you do there, will usually beat a vague image with no hook. You do not need a long message. You need a clear one.

Do not turn the ads off because a week feels quiet, and do not wait for a launch to turn them back on. The point of a daily range is that it is still running on an ordinary Tuesday.
